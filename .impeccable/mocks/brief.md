# Surface brief: KoLearn app shell (all screens)

Mode: Operate (phone-first, adaptive iOS/Android, web secondary). Audience: English speakers learning Korean, quick pickups and longer study, any light. Pinned by user: pastel colors, fun Korean feel, light and airy (not dark or heavy). Logo unchanged. Replaces the earlier Red Sheet world.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every study day weaves one more pastel stripe into a saekdong sleeve, and each tool owns a stripe color. Refuses the indigo SaaS dashboard and the teal-and-red-film look.

OWN-WORLD: Sugar pastels from saekdong hanbok stripes: rose #FFC9D6, butter #FFE9A8, mint #BFEBD6, sky #BCDFFF, lilac #D9C8FF on warm cream ground #FFF9F2; ink #2B2540 for text. One saturated action color (plum-ink #5B3FD0 family) only on pressable things. Flat color fields, no gradients, no glow. Rounded Hangul display face (Jua) for headlines, Noto Sans KR body, system-feel UI labels. Light by default; dark is a soft dusk-plum variant, never black.

STORY: Learner opens the app, sees this week's stripes and today's article in big rounded Hangul, taps a word chip to see its meaning, continues reading, and the day's stripe fills.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone Today. Top: 7-stripe week band (filled days pastel, today pulsing, future outlined). Large title 오늘 / Today. Article headline in display Hangul with key words under pastel marker. Meaning chip. One dominant Continue reading button in the action color. Tool rows with their stripe color. Native tab bar, active tab tinted by tool stripe.
